# Melimi Telugu Word Formation

Place the complete munujerpulu, padagramulu, padanchalamulu, suffixes, reduplication, analogy and derivational rules here.

## Base-category rules

- Noun-based derivation: కాను, మారి, వాను, పాదు and other documented noun/nominal suffixes attach after noun bases. The whole derived word gets its meaning from the base + suffix combination.
- Verb-based derivation: అలవి/అల్వి and అరిది/అర్ది attach to verb bases. Example: చేయు + అలవి -> చేయల్వి.
- Do not apply a noun suffix to a verb or a verb suffix to a noun merely because the suffix exists.
- Existing plural/case inflection rules remain separate from derivational word formation.
- Some non-ం/nasal-ending Melimi lexical forms can be invariant noun/adjective forms. Example: హాళికాను can express both ఆసక్తికరం and ఆసక్తికరమైన according to syntax. No extra adjective suffix is required.
